Reading #2: Schön & Bennett: Reflective Conversation With Materials




This reading sort of linked the idea that a well created product came from someone who had a good idea of what the audience would want. During the stages of development, the creator goes through a bunch of questions such as; “is this the direction I want to continue in with this design?”, “where should I go next?”, “does this really portray what I am trying to say?” etc. These reflections sort of morph the product into what it becomes and the reactions behind what the final product becomes is evermore subjective. There’s a quote however in the text that says: “Not every designer can produce a design that evokes love, but that’s not a bad description of what good design is trying to achieve.” 


The idea that the designer doesn’t necessarily have to produce a fantastic design, but they have to have what the reading called “taste”. This notion can be seen in many ways, my take on it would be that it's a notion that the designer has to have a similar passion and mindset to be able to produce something in the similar “taste” to the audience they are targeting.  


Overall, this text relays that feedback comes in many forms, whenever it is during the process of creation, before or after and how it presents itself. Another notion mentioned was that of “backtalk”, that the design created speaks back to either the creator or the audience differently depending on the work and said audience. In my mind, it's the word given to the feeling you get when you look at not just a design, but for example a painting or any work of art that makes an impression on you. 


It was an interesting read, as it explored the inner workings of the creative process for a designer and the potential challenges that come with it, whether it be physically through trial and error or the feedback reactions of the audience after the fact.
