

Initial ideas

- I want to do an under water walking simulator, which includes a lot of assets in terms of an unwater biome. 


Brainstorming before the process

- Realized that after looking for a good while, I couldn't find a lot of assets without there being a paywall. 
- Went with a couple of assets that were larger instead to make it feel like the user was walking through the bottom of a gigantic ocean/lake. 
- Stuck with that idea going forward


 During the process

- Created a mountainous terrain with a sand material attached to it 
- Used a couple of assets that I scaled up to match the terrain, attaching their materials to themselves as well. 
- One thing I wanted to do but was not able given the time frame was make a filter like effect over the user's vision (not sure how to phrase it) that makes the user feel like they are under water. I really want to be able to accomplish that in the next step to this prototype. 

Thoughts

- As well as potential bubbles or seashells of some sort to collect maybe? 
- Also, as mentioned before, to add a terrain of some sort or an effect that gives rays of light reflecting through the user's vision to truly make the player feel like they are underwater which is the goal of my prototype.
